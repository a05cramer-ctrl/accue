Where every trade goes.

Creator fees are collected in SOL; the Vault applies the split.

  1. 01

    A trade comes in

    Every buy and every sell pays a creator fee in SOL. It drops toward the Vault.

  2. 02

    The bot collects

    Creator fees wait on the pad until collection. The Vault receives SOL when the bot collects the available fees.

  3. 03

    Each collection splits

    60% enters the staking reserve, 20% funds buybacks and 20% is paid to the treasury.

  4. 04

    Staking pays gradually

    The staking reserve releases 40% of its remaining balance per day while tokens are staked. Without stakers, it waits.

Before you buy.

What is the Vault, exactly?

A wallet that collects creator fees from $ACCRUE trading. Each collection splits 60% to the staking reserve, 20% to buyback and burn and 20% to the treasury.

What does a trade cost?

The pad's normal trading fee. The creator-fee part of it is what flows to the Vault. $ACCRUE adds no transfer tax on top.

Where do I buy?

On pump.fun, with the address shown on this page. Copy it from the site and check it against the pinned post on X.

Do I have to do anything to get paid?

Stake and claim. Staking is opt-in: tokens that are not staked earn nothing.

When can I claim?

10 minutes after your last deposit. A top-up restarts the timer.

Can I get my $ACCRUE back whenever I want?

Yes, after the same 10 minutes. The SOL you earned stays claimable even at zero stake.

What if I stake after everyone else?

The rate per token is the same for every staker. You earn from the moment you stake, not from before it.

What happens if trading stops?

No new fees means no new reserve. The reserve keeps releasing what it already holds at 40% a day, a little less each day.

Does the burn make the price go up?

No promise. The burn removes tokens from supply; the price depends on demand.
